The Woodlands is a master-planned community about 27 miles north of Houston along the Interstate 45 corridor, founded in 1974 by George P. Mitchell. It is not an incorporated city: The Woodlands Township, a special-purpose district, levies a property tax and provides parks, fire protection and other services across a mostly unincorporated area of Montgomery County, with the Village of Creekside Park extending into Harris County. Residential villages such as Alden Bridge, Cochran's Crossing and Creekside Park surround a Town Center anchored by The Woodlands Waterway, the Market Street shopping and dining district and the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion, an outdoor amphitheater with a full annual concert season. Housing ranges from detached single-family homes to townhomes and condominiums, most of it governed by recorded covenants that the Township administers. More than 150 parks and over 220 miles of pathways thread through the community's preserved woods.
