Clear Lake is a district in southeast Harris County, largely within the City of Houston following a 1977 annexation, and bordered by separately incorporated cities including Webster and Nassau Bay. Development began in 1963, when the Friendswood Development Company started building a master-planned community to support the growing space program; NASA's Johnson Space Center and its public visitor center, Space Center Houston, remain anchors of the area today. Interstate 45, the Gulf Freeway, is the primary corridor connecting Clear Lake to downtown Houston, roughly 20 to 25 miles to the northwest. The area borders Clear Lake itself and Galveston Bay, with public parks offering boat launches and paddling access, and the Kemah Boardwalk, a waterfront dining and amusement destination, sits nearby in neighboring Kemah. Housing includes single-family subdivisions, townhomes, and condominiums, most organized under community associations tied to the area's master-planned origins.
