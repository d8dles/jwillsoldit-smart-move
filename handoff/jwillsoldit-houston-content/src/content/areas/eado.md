EaDo, short for East Downtown, is a district immediately east of Downtown Houston, in the City of Houston and Harris County. The area developed from the 1890s through the 1930s as a warehouse and light-industrial district served by rail lines; many of those brick warehouse buildings have since been converted into offices, studios and loft-style residences. Newer construction includes townhome subdivisions and multifamily apartment buildings alongside the older industrial stock. The East Downtown Management District coordinates improvements and services within the area. Shell Energy Stadium, home of Houston Dynamo FC and the Houston Dash, sits within EaDo and is served by the METRORail Green and Purple Lines at the EaDo/Stadium station, with a transfer available to the Red Line toward the Texas Medical Center. Active freight rail lines cross parts of the district, a legacy of its industrial history. Houston ISD serves the area.
