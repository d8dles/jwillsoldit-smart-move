Sugar Land is an incorporated city in eastern Fort Bend County, roughly 20 to 25 miles southwest of downtown Houston along US Highway 59/Interstate 69. Originally built around the Imperial Sugar company town, it incorporated in 1959 and grew over the following decades into a series of master-planned sections, including First Colony. Sugar Land Town Square and the Smart Financial Centre anchor its central commercial district, and Constellation Field, home of the Sugar Land Space Cowboys, sits nearby. The city also owns and operates Sugar Land Regional Airport, a general aviation and corporate airport. Housing includes single-family homes in master-planned sections, townhomes, and multi-family communities, with most residential development organized under homeowners or community associations. Land along the Brazos River is protected by a series of levee improvement districts that operate separately from city government.
