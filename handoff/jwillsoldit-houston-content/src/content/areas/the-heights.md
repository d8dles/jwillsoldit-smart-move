The Heights is a neighborhood northwest of Downtown Houston, inside Loop 610, within the City of Houston and Harris County. Founded in 1891 as an independent town, it was annexed by Houston in the late 1910s. Three sections, Houston Heights West, East and South, are now City of Houston historic districts, where exterior changes to a property go through a design review process. Housing includes early twentieth-century bungalows, Craftsman and Victorian-era homes, and newer, higher-density infill construction; lot sizes and setbacks vary from block to block, and some blocks carry separate minimum-lot-size ordinances. The West 19th Street corridor is a commercial strip of shops and restaurants in early-1900s storefronts. The Heights Hike and Bike Trail, built along a former rail line, runs through the area. Interstate 10, Loop 610 and surface streets connect the area to the rest of the city; travel time varies with traffic and time of day.
